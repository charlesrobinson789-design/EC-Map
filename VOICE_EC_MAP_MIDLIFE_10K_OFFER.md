## 2. The Offer

**Who it is for:** professional women aged 40–58 who are in perimenopause or postmenopause and have ADHD-pattern executive strain. That strain may be diagnosed, suspected, or newly louder. These women still look high-functioning, but they are privately losing capacity.

**The problem in her words:** "I can't tell if this is ADHD, perimenopause, burnout, or sleep, and the systems that used to work have stopped working."

**Mechanism (the differentiator):** the EC Map intake separates two things:

- longstanding attention and executive patterns;
- body-state amplifiers, which include sleep, the hormonal transition, recovery debt, and load.

The engagement then builds her operating system around both. It does not treat either one as "the answer."

**Outcome promise (a direction, not a guarantee):** within 6 months she is running work and home on a plan built for the capacity she has now. She plans around her better days and her lower-capacity days instead of being ambushed by them. She also walks into her own clinicians' offices with organized functional data.

### What's Included

| Phase | Timing | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| Map | Weeks 0–2 | Full EC Map intake, then a 90-minute Capacity Map session, then a written Capacity Signature report |
| Build | Months 1–3 | Biweekly 60-minute sessions. She builds a demand/energy calendar, a task-initiation system, work and home load redistribution, and a tracking routine |
| Stabilize | Months 4–6 | Biweekly sessions for stress-testing the system (travel, deadlines, caregiving surges, symptom swings), then a handoff playbook |
| Throughout | 6 months | Async support on weekdays (Voxer or Slack, 24-hour response) and a monthly capacity review |
| Care-Conversation Brief | Month 0, 3, 6 | A functional summary plus questions for **her own** prescriber, OB-GYN, or therapist. It contains no treatment recommendations |

That comes to 12 biweekly sessions plus the Map session, which is about 13.5 hours of live time.

### Effective rate compared with the existing ladder

| Offer | Price | Hours | $/hour |
|---|---|---|---|
| Beta | $500 | ~2.75 | ~$182 |
| Clarity Sprint | $1,000 | ~5 | ~$200 |
| **Midlife Partnership** | **$10,000** | **~40** | **~$250** |

The $10K offer is not only a higher price. It also gives one sale for every 10–20 lower-ticket sales, which means far less time spent selling per dollar.

### Capacity ceiling (the constraint that matters)

The unit-economics workbook budgets 60 founder hours a month. If about 15 hours go to selling and content, 45 hours are left for delivery. Each client takes about 6.7 hours a month, so the practical limit is **6 concurrent clients**. That is about 12 clients a year, or $120K.

Ways to go past about $120K, in order:

1. Raise the price after 5 testimonials.
2. Cap async support.
3. Use a VA for notes and scheduling.
4. Add a small-group tier.

Don't solve for capacity before client #6.

### Payment terms

- Paid in full: $10,000.
- Payment plan: 3 × $3,600, charged monthly by auto-charge. The total is $10,800.
- Credit: the $500 Beta or $1,000 Sprint fee counts toward the Partnership if she enrolls within 30 days.
- Refunds: a full refund within 7 days of payment if the Map session hasn't happened. After that the engagement is non-refundable, and a payment plan is a commitment to the full amount. Put this in the agreement, and log each delivered session as chargeback evidence.

## 4. Who to Sell To First

**Best-fit buyer:**

- She is a director, physician, attorney, founder, nurse leader, or executive, and has discretionary income.
- She was diagnosed with ADHD in midlife, or was diagnosed earlier and is newly struggling. She has either started menopausal hormone therapy and is still struggling, or is weighing it.
- There is a trigger event, such as a performance review, a promotion, a missed deadline, a caregiving surge, or a return-to-work.

**Disqualify or refer out:**

- Active crisis or safety risk: give 988 and emergency resources.
- She wants a diagnosis, medication, or hormone advice: refer her to a prescriber.
- Her primary need is trauma or mood treatment: refer her to therapy.
- Paying would be a financial hardship. Selling $10K into distress drives refunds and complaints.
- **She is a current or recent patient of yours. Never sell the coaching offer to your psychiatric patients.** Doing so creates a dual relationship and board risk.

## 6. Application Questions

The same questions are built into the form on `midlife.html`.

1. Name, email, and state of residence.
2. What's happening right now that made you apply? (free text)
3. Which describes you? Diagnosed ADHD / suspected ADHD / not sure
4. Which stage fits? Perimenopause / postmenopause / not sure / prefer not to say
5. Are you currently working with a prescriber or therapist? (Yes/No. This is for context only and is not required.)
6. What would make the next 6 months a win?
7. The Partnership is a $10,000 investment (a payment plan is available). Is that feasible for you right now? Yes / Yes with the plan / Not right now

Anyone who answers "Not right now" goes to the $500 Beta. That is a downsell, not a lost lead.

## 7. Fit Call (45 Minutes)

| Min | Step | Say or ask |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 | Frame | "My goal is to see whether this is a fit. If it isn't, I'll point you somewhere better. Sound good?" |
| 3–15 | Current state | "Walk me through a typical hard week. What's slipping that nobody else sees?" |
| 15–22 | Cost | "What has this cost you: work, relationships, health, money? What happens if the next 12 months look like this one?" |
| 22–27 | Desired state | "Six months from now, what's different on a Tuesday?" |
| 27–30 | Scope check | "I want to be clear that this is non-clinical. I won't diagnose or advise on medication or hormones. Your clinicians keep that role, and I'll hand you organized data to take to them." |
| 30–38 | Offer | Walk through Map, Build, and Stabilize, plus the briefs and async support. Tie each part to what she said. |
| 38–42 | Price | "The investment is $10,000, or three payments of $3,600." Then stop talking. |
| 42–45 | Close | "Would you like to start? I can send the agreement and payment link now, and we'll book your Map session this week." |

**Red flags that end the call with a referral:** safety concerns, requests for a diagnosis or medication advice, or financial hardship.

## 8. Price Objections

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "That's a lot." | "It is. What has the current pattern cost you this year in time, performance, and health? If the answer is less than $10K, this isn't the right move, and I'll tell you that." |
| "Can I just see my doctor?" | "Yes, and you should. This doesn't replace that. Your doctor manages the medical side, and I build the system you run every day between appointments." |
| "I need to think about it." | "Of course. What specifically do you need to decide? Let's name it now." Then book a follow-up within 48 hours. |
| "I've tried coaching and planners." | "Most of those assume your capacity is constant. We map what drives your good and bad days first and build around that." |
| "Can't afford it right now." | Offer the payment plan. If that still isn't possible, offer the $500 Beta with credit toward the Partnership. |

## 11. Guardrails That Protect the Revenue

| Risk | Action |
|---|---|
| Board of nursing treats the coaching as nursing practice | Operate as a separate LLC. The agreement states that this is non-clinical and that no NP–patient relationship exists. Don't diagnose, treat, or advise on medications or hormones. **Confirm in a one-hour consult with a healthcare attorney in your state before the first sale.** |
| Liability coverage gap | Ask your malpractice carrier in writing whether coaching is excluded. If it is, add a coaching professional liability or E&O policy. |
| Dual relationships | No current or recent psychiatric patients as coaching clients. |
| FTC | No guaranteed health or income outcomes. Testimonials need written consent, and results that aren't typical need a disclosure. |
| Data | Collect the minimum on the application. Don't upload intake content into ad or CRM audiences. Follow the privacy marketing rule in the campaign doc. |
| Crisis | Safety disclosures move out of the coaching container: 988, emergency services, and her clinicians. |
| Copy | Run public copy through `src/copy-rules.ts` (`findCopyRuleViolations`) before posting. |
