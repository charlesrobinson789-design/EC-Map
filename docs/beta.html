Strict Scope & Safety Boundaries

To protect trust and clinical integrity, Voice EC Map operates under conservative guardrails.

What we are (In Scope)

  • Functional education: Mapping cognitive patterns, attentional friction, and executive load.
  • Case organization: Creating structured, objective summaries to make your external clinical appointments more productive.
  • Human-reviewed consulting: Bounded AI agents assist with drafting and sorting, but every final report is reviewed by human oversight.

What we are NOT (Out of Scope)

  • No medical or psychiatric diagnosis: We do not diagnose ADHD, menopause, generalized anxiety, depression, or any DSM-5/ICD-10 condition.
  • No treatment or medication advice: We do not recommend, prescribe, or advise on stimulant medications, hormone replacement therapy (HRT), or medical regimens.
  • No crisis intervention: AI agents are strictly blocked from handling emergency or self-harm disclosures. Critical safety signals automatically trigger professional referral protocols.
