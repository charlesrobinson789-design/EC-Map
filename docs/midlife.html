Accountability every weekday, adjusted to your capacity

Insight isn't the hard part. Doing it on a Tuesday when you slept four hours is. That's why this partnership runs on structure you can't quietly drift away from: a daily check-in, a same-day reply, a weekly scorecard, and a clear plan for missed days. Accountability here means scaffolding, not judgment. On a low-capacity day we shrink the plan to its minimum; we don't drop it.

  • Every weekday: 60-second check-in covering energy, sleep, capacity, and your top 3
  • Same day: a coach reply that adjusts today's plan to today's capacity
  • Every week: a 1:1 with your scorecard and next week's 3 commitments
  • Every month: a capacity review across body-state and demand
  • Missed a check-in? A nudge on day 1, a voice note on day 2, and a 15-minute reset call on day 3. No shame, just structure.

Is this a fit?

A strong fit if you

  • Are in perimenopause or menopause, or suspect you're in the transition
  • Have diagnosed or suspected ADHD, or executive strain that has recently gotten louder
  • Are still functioning at work, but at a private cost
  • Want a structured partner for 6 months, not another app

Not the right fit if you

  • Want a diagnosis, medication, or hormone guidance. Your clinician is the right person for that.
  • Need therapy or crisis support right now
  • Would be under financial strain from the investment. Start with the $500 Beta instead.

Scope and safety

  • Non-clinical and non-diagnostic. This is functional coaching and consulting. It is not a medical or psychiatric evaluation, therapy, or treatment advice.
  • Your clinicians stay in charge of diagnosis, medication, and hormone decisions. The Care-Conversation Briefs are designed to make those appointments more productive.
  • Human-led. Every session and report is delivered by a person. AI tools help only with drafting and organization under human review.
  • No guaranteed outcomes. We build and test systems with you. Results depend on many factors, including health and life circumstances.
  • Not crisis support. If you are in crisis, call or text 988 or call 911.
