The Science & Structure Behind the Map

Why we rely on fixed algorithms instead of LLM inference for scoring, and how our 64-item spine separates lifelong cognitive traits from midlife amplifiers.

The 4-Function Scoring Algorithm

Every response across our 64-item assessment is evaluated across four orthogonal mathematical dimensions.

1. Signal Intensity

Measures the baseline functional friction or symptom frequency in a specific cognitive domain on a standard 0–4 scale.

2. Compensation Cost

Quantifies the hidden energetic and physical toll required to mask difficulty and maintain visible performance standards.

3. Discriminator

Evaluates whether the pattern represents lifelong attentional continuity (ADHD-consistent) or recent midlife emergence.

4. Modifiability

Identifies how sensitive the symptom is to environmental structure, recovery sleep, and schedule modifications.

The Differential Lens: Avoiding the Single-Label Trap

In midlife, cognitive strain is frequently misattributed. Women with longstanding, unspotted ADHD may be told their focus issues are "just menopause." Conversely, women experiencing severe hormonal sleep disruption may suspect sudden-onset ADHD.

Voice EC Map resolves this ambiguity by analyzing setting spread, lifelong patterns, and body-state factors simultaneously. We display both ADHD-consistent and Menopause-amplified signals side by side without forcing a premature conclusion.

Comparative Domain Mapping

  • Timeline anchor: Present since childhood vs. emergent in late 30s/40s.
  • Setting spread: Pervasive across home/work vs. isolated to high-demand environments.
  • Sleep stability: Independent of rest vs. directly correlated with fragmented sleep.
  • Hormonal sensitivity: Stable baseline vs. predictable monthly/perimenopausal volatility.
