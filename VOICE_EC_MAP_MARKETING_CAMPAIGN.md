# Voice EC Map Marketing Campaign

Created: 2026-05-28

## Executive Decision

Launch Voice EC Map as a narrow, high-trust, AI-assisted case management consulting offer:

> A structured midlife capacity mapping service that helps women, coaches, case managers, and care-adjacent professionals organize cognition, body-state, and functional-load signals before a first session, appointment, or care-planning conversation.

The firm should not market itself as a medical provider, diagnostic assessment, therapy product, menopause treatment tool, ADHD evaluator, crisis responder, or autonomous clinical agent. The correct launch position is:

> Human-reviewed consulting, powered by bounded AI agents, using Voice EC Map as the structured intake and report engine.

The campaign should sell a practical outcome:

> Turn scattered midlife cognition stories into a usable capacity map, coach packet, and next-conversation plan.

## AI Agent Operating Model

The firm can be "run by AI agents" only if each agent has a bounded job, data limits, and a human approval gate.

| Agent | Job | Inputs | Outputs | Must Not Do | Human Gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Strategy Agent | Maintain positioning, offers, campaign thesis | product docs, sales notes, analytics | weekly strategy memo | invent clinical claims | founder approval |
| Content Agent | Draft posts, emails, lead magnets | approved message bank | content drafts | publish without review | founder approval |
| Claim Guardrail Agent | Check copy against claim rules | all public copy | pass/fail notes | provide legal advice | founder or counsel review |
| Outreach Agent | Identify referral partners and draft outreach | public profiles, CRM tags | contact list, email drafts | scrape sensitive data or impersonate founder | founder approval |
| Lead Triage Agent | Score inbound leads | form fields, consented notes | lead score, next step | process crisis/safety messages as sales leads | founder review |
| Intake Prep Agent | Prepare intake workflow | client package choice, consent status | intake link, instructions | collect emergency data in public forms | founder review |
| Report Assembly Agent | Draft structured packet | EC Map output, confirmed notes | draft report and coach packet | change scores or safety routing | founder approval |
| Follow-Up Agent | Send approved reminders and next steps | CRM stage, templates | follow-up emails | send medical advice | approved templates only |
| Analytics Agent | Track KPIs | web, CRM, email, sales data | dashboard and weekly memo | expose PHI in reports | de-identified reporting |

## Sales Call Script

### Fit Call Goals

- confirm the person understands this is non-diagnostic
- identify whether the need is direct-client, partner, or practice-buyer
- identify urgency and scope
- avoid collecting unnecessary sensitive detail
- route out-of-scope needs to appropriate support
- offer the right package

### Call Flow

1. "What made you interested in Voice EC Map?"
2. "Are you looking for personal case organization, a coach packet, or a provider/practice workflow?"
3. "What is the main ambiguity: ADHD, menopause, burnout/load, sleep/recovery, medication/substance effects, medical questions, or mixed?"
4. "Do you already have a coach, clinician, therapist, PMHNP, OB-GYN, or care team you plan to discuss this with?"
5. "What would make this worth the cost?"
6. "I want to be clear on scope: this does not diagnose, treat, prescribe, or replace care. It organizes functional information and prepares better questions."
7. "The best-fit package is [package]. The next step is [intake/payment/partner demo]."

### Disqualification / Referral Language

Use when needed:

> This sounds like it needs direct medical, mental health, emergency, or crisis support rather than a case-organization product. Voice EC Map is not the right first step for that situation.

### AI-Agent Claim Rules

Every agent claim must answer:

1. What task does the agent perform?
2. What data does it access?
3. What can it not do?
4. Where is human review required?
5. What happens if sensitive or safety content appears?

### HIPAA / Privacy Marketing Rule

Operational rule:

> Never use assessment-derived information for marketing, retargeting, audience uploads, lookalike audiences, testimonials, case studies, or sales outreach without explicit written permission and legal review.

Public case studies should be:

- synthetic
- de-identified and consented
- or generalized with no unique personal detail

## Public FAQ

### Is Voice EC Map a diagnostic tool?

No. It is a structured functional intake and case-organization service. It may identify ADHD-consistent or menopause-amplified patterns to discuss with a qualified professional, but it does not diagnose.

### Is this therapy or medical care?

No. It is not therapy, medical care, prescribing, hormone treatment advice, or crisis support.

### Why use AI agents?

Agents help with bounded operational tasks: organizing intake data, drafting structured report language, checking claims, sending approved follow-ups, and tracking campaign metrics. They do not make clinical decisions.

### Is Voice EC Map clinically validated?

No. The current product is construct-informed and designed for future validation. Public claims should stay evidence-informed and conservative.

### Who reviews the output?

The solopreneur/founder reviews client-facing outputs before delivery. Agent drafts are not released automatically.

### What if my answers include safety concerns?

Safety content is handled separately from capacity scoring and should route to appropriate human support. Voice EC Map is not an emergency or crisis service.
