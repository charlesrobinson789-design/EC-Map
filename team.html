Licensed PMHNP Expertise

Voice EC Map was originated and structured by a licensed Psychiatric Mental Health Nurse Practitioner (PMHNP) specializing in midlife cognition, ADHD presentations, and functional capacity assessment.

Every domain in our 64-item spine was curated based on clinical reality: separating true attentional continuity traits from transient body-state disturbances and hormonal sleep disruption.

Why Human Oversight is Mandatory

In an era of unchecked generative AI, we refuse to let autonomous models make clinical claims or safety decisions. That is why our Sol MVP architecture ensures that scoring algorithms remain deterministic.

Before any client or coach packet is delivered, it passes through a strict human review gate to verify boundary compliance, claim accuracy, and referral routing.
